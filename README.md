# A Study on Quality of Work Life & Occupational Stress Among Employees

## Overview

This research project focuses on analyzing the relationship between employees’ quality of work life and occupational stress in modern workplaces. The study was conducted using survey-based research methods to understand factors affecting employee satisfaction, workplace well-being, work-life balance, and stress levels.

The project involved preparing questionnaires, creating Google Forms, conducting surveys, collecting employee response data, and analyzing the collected information to identify workplace challenges and generate meaningful insights for improving organizational work environments.

---

## Tools & Technologies Used

* **Microsoft Excel**

  * Data Cleaning
  * Data Organization
  * Exploratory Data Analysis (EDA)
  * Charts & Visualizations

* **SPSS**

  * Statistical Analysis
  * Survey Data Analysis
  * Data Interpretation

* **Google Forms**

  * Survey Creation
  * Data Collection

---

## Objectives

* Examine work-life balance and its impact on employee performance and satisfaction.
* Identify factors influencing employee well-being and workplace satisfaction.
* Analyze workplace relationships and organizational culture affecting employees.
* Evaluate workload and occupational stress factors within organizations.
* Generate insights and recommendations to improve employee work-life quality.

---

## Project Workflow

1. Prepared questionnaires and designed survey forms using Google Forms.
2. Conducted surveys and collected primary employee response data.
3. Organized, cleaned, and structured the collected datasets using Excel.
4. Performed statistical analysis and exploratory data analysis (EDA) using Excel and SPSS.
5. Analyzed employee stress levels, workplace satisfaction, and organizational factors.
6. Presented findings through reports, charts, and visualizations.

---

## Analysis Performed

* Employee Work-Life Balance Analysis
* Occupational Stress Analysis
* Employee Satisfaction Analysis
* Workplace Environment Analysis
* Organizational Culture Evaluation
* Trend and Pattern Identification

## Key Insights

* Identified major workplace stress factors affecting employees.
* Analyzed the relationship between work-life balance and employee satisfaction.
* Evaluated how organizational culture and workload influence employee well-being.
* Observed patterns related to workplace stress and overall job satisfaction.

---

## Outcomes

* Generated meaningful insights into employee workplace experiences and stress factors.
* Improved understanding of factors impacting employee well-being and productivity.
* Provided data-driven findings to support better workplace management practices.
* Highlighted areas where organizations can improve employee satisfaction and work-life quality.

---

## Conclusion

This project provided a comprehensive understanding of the relationship between quality of work life and occupational stress among employees. Through survey-based research, data collection, statistical analysis, and exploratory data analysis using Excel and SPSS, the study identified important workplace factors affecting employee satisfaction, stress levels, and overall well-being. The findings and insights from this project can help organizations create healthier, more supportive, and employee-focused work environments.
